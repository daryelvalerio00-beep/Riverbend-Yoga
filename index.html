Find Your Balance at Riverbend

Riverbend Yoga Studio is a welcoming place for people who want to move, stretch, and make time for themselves. Our classes are designed to support different experience levels, including people who are new to yoga.

You do not need to be flexible or have yoga experience to begin. Our instructors offer guidance and encourage everyone to work at a comfortable pace.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a calm, inclusive space where people can care for their well-being through yoga, movement, and breathing.

Featured Class: Gentle Yoga

A slower-paced class with basic poses, stretching, and breathing. It is a helpful choice for beginners and anyone looking for a gentle practice.
