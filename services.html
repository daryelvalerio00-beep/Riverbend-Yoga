Our Classes and Services

Riverbend offers different class styles so students can choose what feels right for them. Please contact us for current class times and pricing.

Gentle Yoga

A relaxed class with simple movements, stretching, and breathing. This class is suitable for beginners.

Vinyasa Yoga

A flowing class that connects movement with breathing. Instructors offer options so students can adjust the practice.

Restorative Yoga

A slower class focused on rest and comfortable positions, with time to relax and reset.

Private Sessions and Pricing

Private sessions may be requested for students who prefer individual guidance. Class packages and session prices are shared by the studio when you contact us. No online checkout is provided on this website.
