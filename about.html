About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Riverbend Yoga Studio was created to give people a comfortable place to practice yoga and take a break from busy routines. We believe yoga should feel welcoming and accessible, not intimidating.

Our Space

Our studio offers a peaceful setting for classes, workshops, and personal practice. Students are encouraged to ask questions and choose movements that work for them.

Instructors and Accessibility

Our instructors aim to provide clear directions and options for different experience levels. Students can contact us before class to discuss access needs or ask about available accommodations.

Studio Policies

  • Arrive a few minutes before class begins.
  • Wear comfortable clothing and bring water.
  • Let the instructor know if you need an adjustment or alternative movement.
  • Please contact the studio about cancellations and current class policies.
